Who's behind spawnpoint

spawnpoint is built by two builders who got tired of watching great little tools die in a localhost tab. Yes, the red is Rutgers Scarlet: classes of '25 and '26.

ms

Mannan Shukla

Co-founder and CTO

Mannan ships AI infrastructure. He built systems and managed large H100 clusters at Voltage Park, a GPU neocloud. At Rutgers he led RUSLUG, the Rutgers Student Linux User Group. He started spawnpoint so non-technical teams can share the valuable small software they create with agents, and so hobbyists can bring their work to life.

Jack got his start in sneaker automation, shipping bots that beat some of the most hostile anti-bot defenses on the internet. That turned into offensive security: he found vulnerabilities for Rutgers and for Uber. At Rutgers he led the Road to Silicon Valley program. He's responsible for spawnpoint's rock-solid security and reliability.

Why we built it

Every team runs things its own way, and agents can now build the exact tool you need in minutes. But deploying and sharing that tool still meant wrestling a cloud built for software a thousand times bigger. spawnpoint deletes that step. Small software should be as easy to share as a Google Doc.

Want in? The beta is open: sign in with your email and spawn something. We'd love to hear what you build: say hi.
