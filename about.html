Who's behind spawnpoint

spawnpoint is built by two Rutgers builders who got tired of watching great little tools die in a localhost tab. Yes, the scarlet is Rutgers red.

ms

Mannan Shukla

Co-founder · GPU inference @ Lightning AI

Mannan is a software engineer who works on GPU inference at Lightning AI, where he spends his days making AI run fast. He's interested in all sorts of machine learning problems and is, in his own words, always learning and building. He started spawnpoint to fix the last mile of that work: the software agents now write is easy to build but still annoying to deploy and share.

Why we built it

Every team runs things its own way, and agents can now build the exact tool you need in minutes. But deploying and sharing that tool still meant wrestling a cloud built for software a thousand times bigger. spawnpoint deletes that step. Small software should be as easy to share as a Google Doc.

Want in? spawnpoint is in private beta. Say hi, we'd love to hear what you'd spawn.
