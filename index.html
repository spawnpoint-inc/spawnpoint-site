How it works

Three steps from prompt to public link

You do one thing: log in. Your agent does the rest.

1

Grab a token

Sign in, click once, and copy your spk_live_… key.

2

Let your agent build

Point any MCP-capable agent at spawnpoint. It builds your app and calls deploy_project.

3

It spawns

Seconds later, a live public link appears in your console. Share it with anyone.
