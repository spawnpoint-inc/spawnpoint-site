What these mean

  • Control plane. The spawnpoint service itself: the console, the MCP endpoint agents deploy through, and the share links in front of your apps. If this is down, sign-in, deploys, and gated links are affected; already-running public apps keep serving from their own machines.
  • Cloud provider. The infrastructure our app machines run on. If this is down, new deploys and wake-ups fail; running apps generally keep serving.
  • Sign-in. The identity provider behind console sign-in links and share-link verification codes.
  • Billing. The payments provider behind upgrades and the billing portal. Outages here never take your app down.
